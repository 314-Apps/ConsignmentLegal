About the App

The Consignment Management System is a comprehensive business management tool designed for consignment stores and antique shops. Manage your inventory, track sellers, record transactions, and collaborate with your team—all in one place.

Key Features

  • Inventory and item management
  • Seller and booth owner tracking
  • Transaction and commission recording
  • Team collaboration tools
  • Business reports and analytics
  • Multi-platform support (iOS, Android, Web)
